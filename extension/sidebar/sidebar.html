Pinned sites open here. Add one with the + on the rail — the site you're on is pre-filled and you can edit it before saving. Click a pinned icon to open it; right-click to edit or remove.

Snippets & text expander

Right-click any text box → Quick paste to insert a snippet. Give one a shortcut and typing it anywhere expands instantly — e.g. 12# → your phone number.

Cookies

“This session only” accepts cookies so sites work, then wipes them when you close the browser. Set a different rule for any individual site below — open the site, then use “Cookies here”.

Privacy defaults

On first run Sidemorphic turns off Chrome's password saving and autofill for addresses and cards. Change them back any time in Chrome's own settings — it is applied once, not enforced.

Site access

Sidemorphic starts with access to no websites at all. A pinned panel asks your permission for that one site the first time you open it, and nothing else.
